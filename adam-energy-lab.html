Volt's 3 secret rules (say them out loud!)

  1. Energy never disappears. It just changes costume, like chemical → electrical → light.
  2. A circuit is a circle. No gaps allowed, or the electricity stops.
  3. Light goes straight until it hits something. Then it bounces (reflect), bends (refract), or gets eaten (absorb).

Memory trick 🧠

REFLECT = bounces back like a basketball off a wall (mirrors, shiny spoons).

REFRACT = bends when it goes into a new material (a straw looks "broken" in a glass of water).

ABSORB = gets eaten up and turns into heat (black shirt gets hot in the sun).
